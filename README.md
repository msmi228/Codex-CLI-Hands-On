My analytical question: 

 - "How does remodeling status relate to home sale prices, and does this relationship differ depending on the age of the home when controlling for square footage and overall quality?" 

Remodeling status was associated with sale price after accounting for home age, square footage, and overall quality, but the relationship differed with age. 
The results support higher estimated prices for remodeled homes at ages 20 and 50, with a smaller difference for older homes. 
They do not support a higher price for remodeled homes at every age.
One limitation is that the regression did not account for neighborhood, which could be related to both remodeling status and sale price. 
These observational results do not show that remodeling causes a home to sell for more.
